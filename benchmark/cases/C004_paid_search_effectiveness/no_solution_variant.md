# No-Solution Variant Construction Note

## Purpose

Create a realistic paid-search measurement task with rich market and marketing data but no credible source of exogenous variation.

## Removed Or Missing Identification Conditions

- missing_identification_category: exogenous ad-availability variation
- missing_exogenous_variation: No randomized or externally imposed changes in paid-search availability.
- missing_comparison_group: No untreated markets or untreated periods generated by a credible exogenous rule.
- missing_timing_structure: No externally imposed campaign timing shock that can be defended as as-if random.
- missing_outcome_measurement: Outcomes are observed, but clean outcome measurement does not solve endogenous campaign intensity.
- missing_mechanism_separating_contrast: No design feature separates incremental demand creation from interception of existing intent.

## Why Causal Identification Is Not Credible

- core_reason: Campaign intensity, spend, and clicks are jointly determined by expected demand and platform or manager decisions.
- main_unobserved_confounder_or_alternative_explanation: Underlying demand conditions and prior user familiarity drive both paid-search activity and downstream purchases.
- why_standard_controls_are_insufficient: Rich historical controls and segment covariates do not guarantee conditional ignorability when campaign intensity responds to expected performance.
- why_common_designs_such_as_rct_did_iv_or_rdd_are_not_available: There is no random holdout, no valid untreated comparison generated by an exogenous rule, no threshold, and no external shock in the available data.

## Maximum Defensible Claim

- strongest_supported_claim: Descriptive or predictive association between paid-search intensity and downstream sales.
- strongest_supported_analysis_type: Correlational panel analysis, forecasting, or attribution-style decomposition.
- what_should_remain_descriptive_or_predictive_only: Any claim about causal incremental paid-search lift or segment-specific causal response.

## Expected Strong-Agent Response

- state_that_credible_causal_identification_is_not_possible: yes
- propose_descriptive_or_correlational_analysis_only: yes
- specify_what_additional_data_or_design_change_is_needed: randomized ad-availability variation, exogenous market rollout, or another credible quasi-experimental source of variation
- clearly_mark_causal_claims_as_unsupported: yes

## Expected Failure Pattern

- likely_bad_agent_response: Regress sales on spend, clicks, or attributed conversions with many controls and claim causal ROI or causal incremental lift.
- expected_error_types: endogenous-exposure overclaim; observational ROI overclaim; mechanism claim without identification
- [x] No-solution overclaim
- [ ] Invented instrument
- [ ] DID without pre-period or control group
- [ ] RDD without a cutoff
- [ ] RCT claim without random assignment
- [x] Mechanism claim without mechanism-separating evidence

## Scoring Notes

| agent behavior | expected label |
|---|---|
| refuses strong causal identification and proposes descriptive analysis | correct |
| proposes causal OLS or panel regression with controls | overclaim |
| invents randomization or quasi-random exposure timing | contradiction |
| states assumptions but has no credible variation source | weak identification |
