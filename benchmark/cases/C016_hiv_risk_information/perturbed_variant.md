# Perturbed Variant Construction Note

## Purpose

Keep the same school-based information-content setting while removing the key objective downstream outcome.

## Base Identification Condition

- changed_condition_category: outcome measurement structure
- base_condition: The base task includes an objective downstream consequence related to unsafe behavior and treats self-report as supplementary.
- why_it_supports_identification: It reduces dependence on socially sensitive self-reports and makes the content comparison more credible.
- where_it_appears_in_base_task: `agent_task_level2.md` and `agent_task_level3.md` in the main outcome and data-card sections.

## Perturbation

- changed_condition: The objective downstream outcome is unavailable; only later self-reported behavioral outcomes remain.
- variant_file: `agent_task_perturbed.md`
- why_original_strategy_is_weaker_or_invalid: The original design logic relied on objective measurement to avoid reporting-bias problems. Without it, content effects can still be randomized, but the substantive behavioral interpretation is much weaker.
- does_this_weaken_invalidate_or_redirect_the_base_design: weakens and redirects the design toward cautious interpretation of survey evidence.
- which_original_claims_should_no_longer_be_valid: Strong claims that the targeted information causally reduced unsafe behavior in real downstream outcomes.

## Expected Strong-Agent Response

- what_a_good_agent_should_say: Randomization in information content may still identify effects on reported behavior, but the study can no longer make equally strong claims about actual unsafe behavior because the primary outcome is self-reported and selective.
- correct_design_adjustment: Downgrade the causal interpretation of behavioral mechanisms, propose validation against objective records, or propose restoring an administrative or verified outcome.
- claims_that_should_be_weakened: Strong claims about real behavior change, especially on protection or partner choice, if based only on self-report.
- additional_assumptions_or_data_needed: Objective downstream outcome data, validation subsamples, or independent measurement not generated by self-report alone.
- what_should_not_be_claimed: That the perturbed design preserves the same evidentiary strength as the base design.

## Expected Failure Pattern

- likely_bad_agent_response: Reuse the base design and treat survey responses as if they deliver the same credibility as the original objective outcome.
- expected_error_types: mechanical reuse of base design; ignoring changed measurement structure; unsupported mechanism claim
- [x] Mechanical reuse of base design
- [ ] Ignoring changed assignment mechanism
- [ ] Ignoring changed timing structure
- [x] Ignoring changed measurement structure
- [x] Unsupported causal or mechanism claim
